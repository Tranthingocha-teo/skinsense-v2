# SkinSense design: C3 · Night photo

The look for web and Android after the 2026-09 redesign. Values live in `packages/tokens/tokens.json`; this file explains the choices and the rules that tokens cannot carry. Decisions and evidence: `.scratch/skinsense-redesign/grill-2026-09-14-design.md`. Reference canvas: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/1db1e162-10cb-43f7-a84c-d308174395a1 (rows C3). App core screens (canvas 2): https://claude.ai/code/artifact/804de5e7-f8a3-4a3a-97fa-5bf91de0821b. Prototypes: `apps/web/src/app/proto/c3` and `apps/mobile/src/app/proto-c3.tsx` on branch `proto/redesign` (not merged).

## Direction

A skin diary is used at night, alone, in front of a mirror. C3 treats each screen like a quiet night photograph: a full-bleed photo at the top fading into near-black, thin display type, glass cards over the photo and solid cards below it. Picked over C1 (warm dark) and C2 (cool light) after seeing Today, Trends, the landing hero and the mark in both themes.

Known costs, accepted: the look depends on real photography (placeholders today), thin type over photos makes contrast harder, and glass blur costs GPU on Android.

## Themes

Dark is the primary look; light is designed alongside it, never generated by inversion. Both come from `color.dark` and `color.light` in the token file. Every text pair must pass WCAG AA (4.5:1) in both themes; `packages/tokens/build.mjs` fails the build otherwise.

Colour roles: `bg` page, `surface` solid cards, `surface-2` tracks and secondary fills, `fg` text, `muted` secondary text, `line` hairlines, `accent` the one filled action per screen (cream on dark, ink on light, never a hue), `on-accent` text on it, `good` improving trends and routine streaks, `danger` destructive actions only, `photo-a/b/c` the photo placeholder gradient. There is no brand hue: the photo carries the colour.

## Type

Two faces, both with Vietnamese coverage:

- Display: Noto Serif Display, weight 300 (400 for card titles). Chosen over Cormorant Garamond, which the canvas and prototypes used, because Cormorant places the circumflex on ô/ồ/ổ visibly off the letter at every weight, on web and Android alike (comparison: `~/docs/case-study/skinsense/prototype/font-vi-test.png`).
- Body: Manrope, 300–600.

Scale (mobile, px): screen title 54 / line 60, display 300; hero numbers 44–52, display 300; card title 26–28, display 400; body 15–15.5, Manrope 300 (done/secondary) or 400; meta 12.5–13, Manrope 300 in `muted`; labels 11, Manrope 500, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.12em. Web hero headline: uppercase display 300, `clamp(36px, 5.2vw, 74px)`, line-height 1.14. Nothing below 11px.

Load budget: web preloads only the display weights used above the fold; every extra weight or subset preloaded before first paint pushed Lighthouse's simulated LCP out (see Performance).

## Shape and surfaces

- Radius: `control` (4) for buttons, chips and segmented controls; `card` (18) for cards; `pill` for date pills and tick circles. Phone mockups on web use 44.
- Glass cards (surface at 72% opacity, 1px border of `fg` at 14%) only where they sit over the photo. Cards below the photo are solid `surface` with a 1px border of `fg` at 10%.
- One filled `accent` button per screen; secondary actions are outlined (1px `fg` at 55%) or underlined text links (offset 4px).
- Hairline progress (1px) instead of thick bars; zone bars are 1px tracks in `line` filled with `good` (improving) or `fg`.
- Icons: stroke 1.5, round caps and joins. No emoji. Navigation and standalone icons are 23–24px; icons inside a text button, chip or pill follow the text (14–20px, as canvas 2 draws them; review of ticket 16, 15/09).

## Photo

The top of Today and the landing hero is a real night photo (to be shot; no stock, no personal photos of others). Until then a gradient of `photo-a/b/c` stands in and is labelled as a placeholder. Text over the photo sits on a scrim that fades from `bg` at 25–40% to solid `bg`; measure contrast on the composited result, not the raw photo.

## Motion

Motion explains the product's loop (capture → mark → trend); it never decorates. All of it switches off under reduced motion, keeping fades only.

Web landing (GSAP 3.15 + ScrollTrigger + Lenis, scrub 1):
- A 400svh scroll track with a sticky 100svh stage, three equal scenes. Scene 1: headline fades up (0.35 of a scene), phone rises from 115% (`power2.out`, 1 scene), four data cards enter (0.5, stagger 0.12, `power3.out`). Scene 2: numbers count up (0.7, `power1.out`), cards drift upward by 45% × their depth (0.8–1.8), phone settles to scale 0.94. Scene 3: the 7-day sparkline draws (0.8).
- The logo mark draws itself once per load: two circle strokes 1.4s `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` (second 0.25s later), then the dot fades in over 0.4s at 1.2s.

Android (Reanimated 4 + react-native-svg):
- Cards enter with `FadeInDown`, 460ms, stagger 80ms, from 12px above (the canvas `.in` offset), CSS `ease-out` (`cubic-bezier(0, 0, 0.58, 1)`).
- Zone bars fill over 700ms, ease-out cubic, delay 420ms + 80ms per row.
- Routine tick: scale to 0.78 in 90ms, spring back (damping 11, stiffness 320), medium haptic when ticking, light when unticking; the progress line follows in 360ms.
- The mark draws itself on the first open of each app session (same timing as web), drawn as react-native-svg circles with an animated `strokeDashoffset` (Skia would need a native rebuild; ticket 04).

## Performance rules

Learned by measuring the prototypes (Lighthouse 13.4.1, mobile, median of 3):
- No GSAP `pin` on content that holds the LCP element: pinning re-parents the section and repaints the headline as a late LCP entry. Use CSS `position: sticky` with a scrubbed timeline.
- Apply the motion layout from the first paint (`@media (scripting: enabled) and (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`), never by flipping a class after hydration (that measured CLS 0.74).
- GSAP reads a CSS `translateY(%)` as pixels: tweens that start from a CSS offset set `y: 0` alongside `yPercent`.
- Motion code loads in its own client chunk after hydration; the page is complete and readable without it.
- Prototype score: Performance 81, Accessibility 100, Best Practices 100, CLS 0, simulated LCP 5.1s. The current landing scores 72 with LCP 4.2s, so LCP is an app-wide issue (hydration JS and 12 preloaded font files before first paint), still open against the ≥ 90 goal.

## Accessibility

- AA contrast for every text pair in both themes (enforced by the token build).
- Tap targets ≥ 44px; the whole routine row is the target, not the 22px circle.
- Colour never carries meaning alone: trends pair colour with an arrow and words ("↓ đang giảm").
- Decorative motion and mockups are `aria-hidden`; counters show their final value in the markup.

## Tokens

`packages/tokens/tokens.json` is the single source (hex, sRGB). `node packages/tokens/build.mjs` checks contrast and writes `packages/tokens/dist/web.css` (CSS variables under shadcn names for Tailwind v4) and `packages/tokens/dist/nativewind.css` (HSL triplets for NativeWind 4 / Tailwind 3). Both apps import them globally since redesign ticket 01 (15/09), so palette, radius and fonts switch at once; each screen's layout switches over when it is rebuilt from the spec (built on `main` screen by screen). The app `dev`/`start` scripts run the token build first because `dist/` is gitignored.
